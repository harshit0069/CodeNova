# FIXLOOP – Smart Complaint & Maintenance Management System

**Project Type:** Web-Based Application  
**Department:** Computer Science and Engineering  
**Technology:** HTML, CSS, Java, Spring Boot, MongoDB

### 1. Introduction
FixLoop is a web-based complaint management system designed for colleges, hostels, and residential buildings. It provides a centralized platform for reporting, tracking, assigning, and resolving maintenance complaints.

### 2. Problem Statement
Traditional complaint management systems face several problems:

- **Lost Complaints:** Verbal or paper-based complaints may be forgotten.
- **No Tracking:** Students cannot check their complaint status.
- **Duplicate Complaints:** Multiple students report the same issue separately.
- **Delayed Resolution:** Lack of proper communication causes delays.
- **No Accountability:** It is difficult to identify who is responsible for resolving an issue.

### 3. Proposed Solution
FixLoop solves these problems by providing:

- **Online Complaint Registration:** Students report issues digitally.
- **Real-Time Status Tracking:** View complaint progress and updates.
- **Duplicate Detection:** Identify similar complaints based on location and description.
- **Task Assignment:** Admin assigns complaints to technicians.
- **Complaint History:** Maintain records of submitted and resolved issues.

### 4. Why Are We Creating This Project?
The aim is to replace inefficient manual complaint processes with a transparent, organized, and accessible digital system.

It will help reduce duplicate reporting, improve communication, simplify complaint tracking, and increase accountability in maintenance operations.

### 5. Expected Outcome
A functional web application where students, administrators, and technicians can manage maintenance complaints efficiently through a single platform.
